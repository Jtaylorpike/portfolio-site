# 3D Gallery Public Review Checklist

Use this checklist after gallery curation or public Three.js gallery changes.

## First impression

- The space feels like a real room, not a void or generic tech demo.
- The mood sits between museum and private archive.
- The photography remains the primary subject.
- The room does not feel like a game level.

## Environment

- Ceiling is visible and helps the room feel enclosed.
- Lighting supports the work without flattening it.
- Fog/haze remains off unless intentionally enabled as a quality/performance option.
- Floor, wall, trim, and plaque details feel intentional.

## Navigation

- WASD/arrow movement feels stable.
- Mouse look feels controlled.
- Movement bounds do not let the camera clip through major walls.
- Wall collision does not block comfortable viewing distance.

## Artwork and curation

- Active gallery walls show the expected assigned artwork.
- Hidden/inactive walls do not accidentally show work.
- Wall block type scale feels appropriate in the room.
- Plaques are readable and feel integrated with the wall/trim system.
- The room still has visual rhythm: anchor moments, standard display moments, and quieter transition moments.

## Future considerations

- Does this wall layout still support a museum/private-archive direction?
- Would a window/time-of-day concept improve the room, or would it distract from the work at this stage?
- Are there any walls that should become real architectural elements rather than simple display blocks?
